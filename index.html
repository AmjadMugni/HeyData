How it works

Four steps, no surprises

You'll know what's happening at each stage, and what you're getting at the end of it.

  1. 01

    Brief & audit

    You send the data and the banner plan. I open the file, check the structure against the questionnaire, and come back with anything that looks off — usually within a day.

  2. 02

    Clean & validate

    Cleaning, logic checks and QC. You get the clean file plus a short note listing what was removed, flagged or recoded.

  3. 03

    Weight & tabulate

    Weighting to your targets, then the full banner set with significance testing — in your table format.

  4. 04

    Charts & handover

    Chart pack in your template, final files, and a walkthrough of anything worth flagging in the data.

About

One person, the whole pipeline

I'm Amjad, a research analyst based in India. For over six years I've worked on quantitative primary research — data processing, tabulation and reporting for agencies running trackers, U&A studies, segmentations and brand work.

I work the way an in-house DP team works, but as a single named person. You brief one person, that person does the work, and that person answers your questions about it.

FAQ

Common questions

Which tools do you work in?

SPSS, WinCross, Q, Decipher, Power BI and Excel. I deliver in your format — if your tables come out of a specific system, send me an old set and I'll match it.

What's your turnaround?

Small jobs — cleaning, a short banner set — usually 2 to 3 working days. Full studies with weighting and a chart pack, 5 to 10. Rush work is possible if you tell me early.

How do you handle messy data?

Audit first. I'd rather spend an hour understanding the structure than fix the same problem four times later. You get a written note of every decision made on the file.

Do you work white-label?

Yes, by default. Your template, your branding, no mention of me anywhere in the deliverable.

What about confidentiality?

Your data stays on one encrypted machine. It never goes into any cloud AI tool. I delete project files 30 days after handover, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before you send anything.

How do you price?

Per project, not hourly. Tell me the number of completes, the banner size and what you need at the end, and you'll get a fixed quote — usually the same day.

What do you need to start?

The data file, the questionnaire and the banner plan. If the banner plan doesn't exist yet, I can draft one from the questionnaire.
